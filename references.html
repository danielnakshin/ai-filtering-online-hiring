How to read the evidence

Claims about specific laws, enforcement actions, and civil-rights guidance rely on official public-agency sources. Historical trade publications and commentary help describe how ATS products evolved, but they should not be treated as independent proof of a universal adoption rate or a universal percentage of resumes rejected before human review.
